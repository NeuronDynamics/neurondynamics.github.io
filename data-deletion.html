User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 19, 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform Terms and data protection regulations (GDPR/CCPA), NimbleBI (operated by Neuron Dynamics) provides users with full autonomy to request the permanent deletion of their personal information and conversation records.

Summary: You can request complete deletion of your WhatsApp phone number, profile identifier, and query interaction history at any time without fees.

How to Request Data Deletion

You may submit a data deletion request using either of the following methods:

  1. Direct Email Request:

    Send an email to 420558513@qq.com with the subject line "NimbleBI Data Deletion Request". Please include the WhatsApp phone number or channel user ID associated with your account so we can locate your records.

  2. In-Chat Command (WhatsApp):

    In your conversation with the NimbleBI WhatsApp bot, type and send /delete-my-data. The automated system will prompt for confirmation and flag your session data for immediate purging.

Data Deletion Timeline and Verification

  • Processing Window: Data deletion requests received via email are reviewed and executed within 7 business days. Automated in-chat requests are processed within 24 hours.
  • Scope of Deletion: All associated personal identifiers, WhatsApp phone numbers, and query records stored in volatile cache and analytical databases will be permanently purged or anonymized.
  • Confirmation: Once your data has been deleted, a confirmation email or message with a unique confirmation tracking ID will be delivered to you.

Questions?

For questions regarding your data privacy rights or our deletion procedure, please contact our data team at 420558513@qq.com.
