Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

NEURON DYNAMICS LIMITED (元動有限公司) ("we", "our", or "us", trading as Neuron Dynamics) operates SafeFlow, an intelligent conversational business intelligence and workflow automation platform integrating messaging channels such as WhatsApp Business API and cloud data platforms. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal and business data transparently and securely.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with SafeFlow via WhatsApp or other supported channels, we may collect the following information:

  • Channel Identifier & Phone Number: Your WhatsApp phone number, display name, and unique user identifier provided by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • Messages and Query Data: The queries, commands, and business prompts you submit to interact with data workspaces and generate insights.
  • Technical and Usage Metrics: Timestamp of interactions, response latency, delivery status, and system performance logs to maintain service reliability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Process your natural language inquiries and deliver requested business intelligence queries and data visualizations.
  • Maintain session continuity and contextual conversational history during your active queries.
  • Authenticate authorized workspace users and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable laws, Meta Developer Policies, and platform terms.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information or business data to third parties.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

In providing the service, data may be processed through:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Cloud API): To transmit messages between you and SafeFlow. Data transmitted via WhatsApp is subject to Meta's WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud Infrastructure Providers: Secure hosting environments adhering to industry-standard encryption and security benchmarks.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit and restricted access policies. Query session data is retained only for the duration required to provide ongoing analytical services or as requested by the enterprise customer.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

Under applicable privacy regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA, and Meta Platform Terms), you have the right to access, rectify, or request the complete deletion of your personal data and message history stored on SafeFlow. For specific step-by-step guidance on deleting your data, please review our User Data Deletion Instructions.
